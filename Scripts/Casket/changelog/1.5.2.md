# 1.5.2

- fixed: `[verification]` lines reuse the same names as `[settings]`/
  `[security]` (e.g. `ransomwareProtection`) but mean something
  different there — whether toggling the setting requires the
  passphrase, not whether the setting itself is on — and looked
  contradictory side by side with no explanation. Added a one-line
  note under the `[verification]` header in both `info` and `settings
  verification state` clarifying what enabled/disabled means there.
