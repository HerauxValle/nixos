# 1.9.2

- added: `create --integrity`/`--no-integrity` — a fresh vault can be
  formatted with dm-integrity from the start, no migration needed
  (there's nothing to copy yet). When neither flag is given and the
  passphrase is being asked interactively, `create` prompts: "Enable
  file integrity protection?", defaulting Enter to yes under 20 GiB and
  no at or above it — the initial device wipe integrity requires gets
  slow on large vaults, so the default doesn't surprise anyone with a
  multi-minute wait on a big one.
