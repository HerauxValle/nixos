# 1.15.1

- fixed: `exec` silently gave a sandboxed process the host's real,
  unrestricted network with no warning at all when `net` was disabled
  after `outbound`/`inbound` had already been enabled (narrowing
  `namespaces` after the fact) — previously only the "net active but
  outbound off" case logged anything. `exec` now always logs one of
  three explicit states at session start: unrestricted (no `net`),
  loopback-only outbound with any active inbound forwards listed, or
  real outbound connectivity active.
