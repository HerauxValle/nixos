cas <vault> settings backup auto enable [--keep N] | disable | keep <N> | state   [--pass "..."]

Automatic snapshot-on-open policy. 'enable' (optionally with --keep N,
default 3) creates a timestamped read-only snapshot every time the
vault is opened, pruning down to the keep count. 'disable' stops future
auto-snapshots (existing ones are kept). 'keep <N>' changes the limit
without re-enabling. 'state' shows the current setting and keep count.

EXAMPLES
  cas myvault settings backup auto enable --keep 5
  cas myvault settings backup auto keep 10
  cas myvault settings backup auto disable
  cas myvault settings backup auto state
