cas <vault> settings encryption enable [--pass "..."]

Requires a passphrase again on 'open'/'toggle', undoing 'disable'. The
vault stays LUKS-encrypted on disk regardless of this setting -- it only
controls whether 'open' prompts. Re-derives and verifies the real
passphrase as part of enabling, so this always self-verifies even if
'settings verification encryption' is off (it's off by default).

EXAMPLES
  cas myvault settings encryption enable --pass "mypass"
