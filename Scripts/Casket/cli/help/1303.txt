cas <vault> settings 2fa enable [--pass "..."]

Generates a keyfile at <vault-dir>/<name>.key (64 random bytes, fixed
path) and re-keys the vault so opening it needs BOTH the passphrase AND
that keyfile -- the real LUKS passphrase becomes
SHA256(your_passphrase + keyfile_contents); neither alone can open the
vault. Back the keyfile up -- losing it means losing access.

EXAMPLES
  cas myvault settings 2fa enable --pass "mypass"
